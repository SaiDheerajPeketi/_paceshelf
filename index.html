Privacy policy

PaceShelf is published by Black and Blue. This policy applies to the PaceShelf Android app and this website.

Your documents stay on your device. PaceShelf processes imported files, recognized text, generated audio, bookmarks, and reading progress locally. Black and Blue does not operate a PaceShelf document-storage or account server.

Information processed on your device

PaceShelf may access files you deliberately select, including PDFs and audio. It stores library metadata, extracted or recognized text, generated audio, reading and listening progress, bookmarks, preferences, and trial state in the app's private storage. Android may also retain files you explicitly export.

Information processed by service providers

  • Google Play processes installation, licensing, purchases, payments, refunds, and subscription management under Google's terms.
  • RevenueCat processes an anonymous app-user identifier, product and entitlement status, purchase history, and limited device/app diagnostics needed to provide and troubleshoot billing. PaceShelf does not send document content or titles to RevenueCat.
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics process privacy-scoped app events, app-instance and device/app information, masked-network-derived coarse country or region, crash traces, and diagnostics so we can understand reliability. PaceShelf does not attach document names, document text, OCR output, bookmarks, or audio content to these events.
  • Google ML Kit performs text recognition on the device. Google's SDK may process limited configuration and diagnostic information as described in Google's ML Kit and Play data-disclosure documentation; PaceShelf does not upload selected document pages or recognized text to a Black and Blue server.

Sharing, selling, and advertising

We do not sell personal information. PaceShelf contains no third-party advertising and disables advertising-ID collection and ad-personalization signals. Information is disclosed only to the processors above, when a user asks us to provide support, or when legally required.

Security and international processing

Local app data is protected by Android's application sandbox and device security. Network traffic to Google, Firebase, and RevenueCat uses encrypted transport. No system is perfectly secure. These providers may process information in countries other than yours under their own legal safeguards.

Children and teenagers

PaceShelf is intended for people aged 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information through a PaceShelf service provider, contact us so we can investigate and request deletion where possible.

Your choices

You can avoid optional document processing by not importing a file, manage or cancel subscriptions through Google Play, delete local data as described below, and contact us about information associated with PaceShelf's service-provider identifiers.

Google Play Data Safety summary

The final Play Console form must be checked against the exact release build. For the current candidate:

  • Files and document content are user-selected and processed locally; they are not sent to Black and Blue.
  • Purchase history is collected by RevenueCat and Google Play to provide purchases, subscriptions, entitlement verification, fraud prevention, and support.
  • App interactions, app-instance and device/app information, masked-network-derived coarse country or region, crash logs, and diagnostics are collected by Firebase for analytics, reliability, and security.
  • Data in transit to service providers is encrypted.
  • There are no ads, advertising-ID collection, or sale of user data.

This website is the controlling public disclosure if store copy is abbreviated.

Data deletion

Delete local PaceShelf data

  1. Delete individual books inside PaceShelf to remove their stored metadata and app-managed files.
  2. To remove all app data, use Android Settings → Apps → PaceShelf → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall PaceShelf.
  3. Delete separately exported files from the location where you saved them.

Request service-provider deletion

Email developer@blackandblue.co.in with the subject PaceShelf privacy request. Include the minimum information needed to locate the record, such as the anonymous app-user identifier shown by support tooling or a Google Play order identifier. Never email document content.

We will request deletion from systems we control or can administer. Google Play may retain payment, tax, fraud, dispute, or legal records. RevenueCat and Firebase may retain limited records for security, compliance, or backup cycles. Deleting billing records does not itself cancel a subscription; cancel it in Google Play.
